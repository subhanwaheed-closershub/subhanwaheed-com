Anti-spam and acceptable use policy

This page is published so anyone, including blocklist operators and abuse desks, can see exactly how this domain is used and how to reach me about it.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Who operates this domain

subhanwaheed.com is operated by Subhan Waheed as a personal site. Contact: subhan@subhanwaheed.com

What this domain is used for

Two things only: a link to my LinkedIn profile, and my personal booking page at book.subhanwaheed.com. That is the whole of it.

No email is sent from this domain for marketing, outreach, or bulk purposes. It hosts no offers, no landing pages, and no redirects into marketing funnels.

Business email I do send

I run business-to-business outreach, and I want to be straightforward about it rather than hide it. That mail is sent from separate dedicated domains, never from this one. Wherever I send it, it follows the same rules:

  • Contact data comes from licensed B2B data providers. Never from scraping, harvesting, or bulk address lists bought from brokers.
  • Truthful sender identity and subject lines. No forged or deceptive headers.
  • A real, monitored reply-to address on every message.
  • A clear way to opt out in every message.
  • Opt-outs honoured within 24 hours, permanently, with no confirmation step and no required wording. Someone asking to stop, in any form, is enough.
  • Anyone who opts out goes on a permanent suppression list and is never contacted again from any domain I operate.

Unsolicited email policy

This website must not be advertised using unsolicited bulk or commercial email, by me or by anyone acting on my behalf, including affiliates, resellers and any third party. Messages referencing subhanwaheed.com may only be sent to recipients with a prior business relationship or who have explicitly requested contact. Advertising this website through unsolicited messages is prohibited, and any sender doing so is acting without authorisation.

Reporting abuse

If you believe this domain, or any message referencing it, has been used abusively, email subhan@subhanwaheed.com. I read every report and reply within two business days. Confirmed abuse means the sending identity involved is shut off immediately.

Compliance

I operate in line with the CAN-SPAM Act, and with GDPR and PECR where they apply, including honouring opt-out and erasure requests.

Changes
